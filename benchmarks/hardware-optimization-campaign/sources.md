# Sources

No external performance claim has been promoted. Current changes are based on local profiling and paired benchmarks against llama.cpp b10079.

- 2026-07-21: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/23903 - reports increased MTP compute-buffer footprint after backend sampling was introduced and names PR 23287 as the prime suspect. Applicability to RTX 3090 throughput is unverified; motivates R001.
- 2026-07-21: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/20700 - WIP FastMTP prototype reports the full vocabulary projection as its largest draft bottleneck and uses a row view to trim draft logits. Its 32K quality claim is not accepted as evidence; K005 tests a conservative 65K view locally with exact-output gates.
- 2026-07-21: https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/438532573807771649/1529351342777303111/message.txt - user-supplied private ROCmFPX campaign report. Portable mechanisms tested locally were direct SSM convolution state, recurrent projection fusion, and staged Q8 activation reuse. AMD-specific wave64, ROCmFP4, HIP launch, and workgroup conclusions were not transferred to CUDA.
- 2026-08-01: https://docs.nvidia.com/nsight-systems/UserGuide/ - current Nsight Systems guidance on CUDA graph tracing, Windows WDDM tracing, and profiling overhead. Used to keep profiler results diagnostic rather than benchmark evidence.
- 2026-08-01: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions/23738 - adaptive MTP depth report. Different Apple hardware and therefore unverified locally; reinforced testing MTP controls only through matched local runs.
- 2026-08-01: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/23184 - proposed pipelined MTP/ngram speculation for repetitive workloads. Not used because the fixed coding prompt and current independent implementations do not establish an equivalent safe gain.
- 2026-08-01: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/docs/speculative.md - upstream speculative-decoding documentation confirms that draftless methods take precedence when combined with a draft model. M083 locally rejected independent `draft-mtp,ngram-mod` at 155.27 tok/s.
- 2026-08-01: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/25187 - current research on MTP draft-vocabulary trimming still scatters compact draft logits back into a full-vocabulary tensor for downstream compatibility. This supports treating full target projection/sampling fusion as new architectural work, not an existing reusable path.
- 2026-08-03: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/25187 - revisited after P180 localized the sequential Q6_K draft head. Its compact-head plus full-logit-scatter design was applied to the actual Qwen3.6 A3B MoE MTP graph and then qualified locally; its external performance numbers were not used as evidence.
- 2026-08-03: https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-GGUF - official standalone Q4_0 MTP draft asset for the exact architecture. Locally verified at 229.004 tok/s and rejected versus the integrated draft.
- 2026-08-05: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/25187 - current draft-vocabulary trimming research reports that the MTP LM head remains a major sequential cost and retains full-logit scatter for compatibility. The local fork already implements and qualifies this mechanism; the report is a lead for profiling the remaining scatter and verification path, not performance evidence.
- 2026-08-05: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions/25198 - current MTP p-min discussion reports workload-specific gains. The local production value remains fixed until a paired five-seed experiment proves a new value across the full matrix.
- 2026-08-05: https://docs.nvidia.com/nsight-compute/NsightCompute/index.html - current Nsight Compute guidance identifies Memory Workload Analysis as the supported view for cache, request, and transfer behavior. Used to select counters for the RTX 3090 kernel follow-up.
- 2026-08-06: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/docs/build.md - current CUDA build guidance documents CUDA_SCALE_LAUNCH_QUEUES. The recommended 4x setting was tested locally in R1218 and rejected.
- 2026-08-06: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/commit/e1a1abb78746c025f5e9039f590e37ccdb758ae7 - upstream transpose-free 1xK GEMV dispatch. It produced a faster but different fixed-seed trajectory locally and was rejected under the exact-output contract.
